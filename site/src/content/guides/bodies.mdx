## Your bodies in the browser

Every step set to **New** makes a body: an extrude, a revolve or a primitive. Fillet numbers them **Body 1**, **Body 2** and so on. Tap <Ui>Browser</Ui> at the left end of the tool strip to see them.

<Figure src="/screens/bodies/body-row-menu.png" width={1400} height={1167} alt="The browser lists Body 1 with a teal icon, Body 2 greyed out with a crossed-out eye, and Body 3 lifted with its menu open: Color, with a tick on Slate, then Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Blue, Purple, Pink, Brown, White and Black, each with its own dot. In the view, a teal plate holds a grey ball." caption="Body 1 is teal, Body 2 is hidden, and Body 3's colours are open." />

- **Tap a row** to select the body. Tap it again to let go.
- **Tap the eye** to hide or show the body. The Print check and exports leave hidden bodies out.
- **Touch and hold a row** to give the body a colour.

## Colour a body

Colours tell bodies apart on screen, so you can see which one is which when they overlap.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Touch and hold the body's row in the browser.
    2. Tap a colour. **Slate** is the default grey.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Control-click the body's row in the browser.
    2. Choose a colour. **Slate** is the default grey.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

The row's icon and the body in the view take the colour. Colours stay on screen and don't go into exported files.

## Add a primitive

A primitive is a ready-made solid. You don't need a sketch for it.

1. Tap <Ui>Primitive</Ui> in the tool strip.
2. Choose **Box**, **Cylinder**, **Sphere**, **Cone** or **Torus**.
3. Type its sizes and its **Position** on the plane.
4. Tap **Done**.

| Shape | Sizes | Where it sits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Box | Width, Depth, Height | Stands on the plane |
| Cylinder | Diameter, Height | Stands on the plane |
| Cone | Bottom Diameter, Top Diameter, Height | Stands on the plane. A top of 0 makes a point. |
| Sphere | Diameter | Centred on the plane |
| Torus | Major Diameter, Minor Diameter | Centred on the plane |

**Plane** is **XY (bed)**, **XZ (front)** or **YZ (side)**. **Operation** works as it does for [an extrude](/guides/extrude-revolve-press-pull/#choose-what-the-extrude-does): **Cut** with a cylinder makes a quick round hole in every body it passes through.

<Tip title="Half under the bed?">A sphere or torus is centred on its plane, so on the bed half of it sits below zero. Lift it with **Move**, Z set to half its height.</Tip>

## Combine bodies

Combine cuts one body with another, or joins them. The **target** is the body that stays. The **tool** is the body that cuts or joins it.

1. Tap <Ui>Combine</Ui>.
2. Under **Target**, choose the body to keep.
3. In the view, tap the tool body. Tap it again to take it out.
4. Choose **Join**, **Cut** or **Intersect**, then tap **Done**.

| Operation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Join | The target and the tools become one body. |
| Cut | The tools are cut out of the target. |
| Intersect | Only the parts where they overlap are kept. |

To keep the tool as well, for a pin that also cuts its own hole, turn on **Keep tool bodies**.

<Note>Select bodies before you tap Combine, and the first one becomes the target and the rest become tools.</Note>

## Move and rotate bodies

Move places bodies by exact distances and angles. You type every value.

1. Select the bodies to move. With nothing selected, Move takes every body.
2. Tap <Ui>Move</Ui>, or press <Keys combo="⇧⌘M" />.
3. Under **Translate**, type the **X**, **Y** and **Z** distances in millimetres.
4. Under **Rotate**, pick an **Axis** and type an **Angle**, then tap **Done**.

Rotation comes before the move. To turn a body in place, rotate it while it sits on the origin, and move it in a second Move. Tap a body in the view to add it or take it out while Move is open.

## Mirror bodies

Mirror copies bodies to the other side of a plane through the origin. Draw one half beside the origin, and Mirror makes the other.

1. Select the bodies to mirror. With nothing selected, Mirror takes the newest body.
2. Tap <Ui>Mirror</Ui>.
3. Choose the **Plane**. **YZ (side)**, the default, mirrors left to right.
4. Tap **Done**.

## Bodies from elsewhere

A STEP file you import arrives as a body named after the file. Fillet it, cut it, combine, move and mirror it like a body you drew. An AI agent can also add **Scale** and **Loft** steps. Tap one in the timeline to change its values.

## Questions

<FAQ>
  <QA q="Why is Combine greyed out?">It needs two bodies. Add another one, a primitive for example, and try again.</QA>
  <QA q="Where did my tool body go?">Combine uses it up. Tap the Combine step in the timeline and turn on **Keep tool bodies**.</QA>
  <QA q="Why does my export miss a part?">The part is probably hidden. Tap its eye in the browser to show it.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I drag a body into place?">No. Move takes typed values, so every position is exact and you can change it later in the timeline. Fields take parameters too, like `wall * 2`.</QA>
</FAQ>
