## Cut the part open

Pick a plane, and Fillet slices the part along it. The half facing you is taken away.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap <Ui icon="tool-section">Section</Ui> at the right end of the tool strip.
    2. Tap **XY**, **XZ** or **YZ** in the bar at the top, or tap a flat face of the part.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click <Ui icon="tool-section">Section</Ui> on the tool strip, choose **View › Section View**, or press <Keys combo="⇧⌘X" />.
    2. Click **XY**, **XZ** or **YZ** in the bar at the top, or click a flat face of the part.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

<Figure src="/screens/section-view/plane-bar.png" width={600} height={136} maxWidth={380} alt="The plane buttons in the bar at the top of the view: XY, XZ and YZ, then Cancel." caption="The bar at the top asks where to cut." />

Solid material is filled with hatching, so holes, pockets and thin walls stand out as gaps.

<Figure src="/screens/section-view/cut.png" width={1236} height={840} maxWidth={680} alt="A plate cut open along XZ through two M3 counterbored holes. <Note>Already selected a flat face? Section cuts right on it, without asking.</Note>

## See the cut as a drawing

Turn on **2D section view** to see the cut flat and square-on, like a section on a technical drawing.

<Figure src="/screens/section-view/2d.png" width={1612} height={376} alt="The plate in 2D section view: a hatched red strip with two T-shaped gaps where the counterbored holes are, a 0 mm value and the round flip button." caption="The counterbores at their true shape, ready to check against a screw." />

- The bed goes away, and the rest of the part is drawn as lines.
- Move the cut with the value on it, or with the panel. The arrow and grips hide in 2D.
- **Section only** shows the cut faces alone, in 2D or 3D.
- Turn 2D off to get your old view back.

<Note>On a Mac, the **View** menu has **2D Section View**, **Section Only** and **Flip Section Side** while a cut is on.</Note>

## Put the handles away or turn it off

Tap **Done** or press <Keys combo="Esc" />. The handles go away, the part stays cut, and this pill shows at the top of the view.

<Figure src="/screens/section-view/pill.png" width={680} height={118} maxWidth={380} alt="A pill reading Section view, with Edit and Turn off buttons." caption="Your reminder that the part is cut, not broken." />

- **Edit** brings the handles and the panel back.
- **Turn off** shows the whole part. So do <Ui icon="tool-section">Section</Ui> and <Keys combo="⇧⌘X" />.
- **Delete** in the panel forgets the plane. Otherwise Section opens on the same cut next time.

<Tip title="Printing tip">Before you print, slide the cut through each screw hole and switch to 2D. You see at a glance whether the counterbore is deep enough for the head and how much wall is left around it.</Tip>

## Questions

<FAQ>
  <QA q="Does a section change my design?">No. It isn't a step, it isn't saved with the file and it isn't in undo. Exports and the Print check always use the whole part.</QA>
  <QA q="Why is half my part missing?">Section view is on. Tap **Turn off** on the pill at the top of the view, or press <Keys combo="⇧⌘X" />.</QA>
  <QA q="Which plane is which?">**XY** is the bed and cuts the part level. **XZ** is the front plane and **YZ** the side plane. Both cut straight down.</QA>
  <QA q="What if the plane misses the part?">The cut moves to the middle of the part instead. The value on the cut says how far that is from the plane.</QA>
  <QA q="Why doesn't tapping the model move the cut?">While the handles are up, taps on the model do nothing, so a stray tap can't move the cut. To cut somewhere else, tap **Change** in the panel.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I type a sum or a parameter?">Yes. Like every field in Fillet, Offset and the tilts take sums and parameter names.</QA>
  <QA q="Why are the cut faces different colours?">Each part gets its own colour and hatch angle (red, then slate, plum, ochre, teal and olive), so parts nested inside each other stay apart. A part with a colour of its own keeps it.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I keep working with the cut on?">Yes. [Measure](/guides/measure/), a sketch or any other tool keeps the cut, and the handles make way. Faces on the cut-away side can't be picked, so you can reach faces inside the part.</QA>
  <QA q="Why is Section greyed out?">There's no body to cut yet, or a step is open. Make a body first, or finish the step with **Done** or **Cancel**.</QA>
</FAQ>
