## Round or bevel edges

Fillet rounds an edge and Chamfer cuts it off at an angle. Both work the same way.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap <Ui icon="fillet-chamfer-shell">Fillet</Ui> in the tool strip, or press <Keys combo="F" />. For a bevel, tap **Chamfer**.
    2. Tap an edge. The edges that run on smoothly from it light up too.
    3. Type a size in **Radius**, or **Distance** for a chamfer.
    4. Tap **Done**.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click <Ui icon="fillet-chamfer-shell">Fillet</Ui> in the tool strip, or press <Keys combo="F" />. For a bevel, click **Chamfer**.
    2. Click an edge. The edges that run on smoothly from it light up too.
    3. Type a size in **Radius**, or **Distance** for a chamfer.
    4. Press <Keys combo="↩" />, or click **Done**.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

The part shows the round as you type, so you see the size before you commit. A new fillet starts at 1 mm and a new chamfer at 0.5 mm.

Edges already selected? Tap **Fillet edges** or **Chamfer edges** under **Next** in the inspector, and the step opens with them picked.

<Note>One step rounds edges on one body. For a second body, make a second step.</Note>

## Pick the edges you want

Each tap adds an edge, and a tap on a picked edge drops it. The blue card counts what you have.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap one edge of an outline. The whole smooth run lights up: "8 edges picked, In 1 tangent chain".
    2. Tap <Ui>Box select</Ui>, or press <Keys combo="W" />.
    3. Drag from right to left across more edges. Everything the box touches is added.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click one edge of an outline. The whole smooth run lights up: "8 edges picked, In 1 tangent chain".
    2. Hold <Keys combo="⇧" /> and drag from right to left across more edges. Everything the box touches is added.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

A few more ways to pick:

- **One edge out of a run:** turn **Tangent chain** off, then tap the edge to drop it. The other edges stay picked.
- **Every edge around a face:** double-tap an edge, on the side of that face.
- **Only what's inside the box:** drag from left to right instead.
- **Without Box select:** draw the box with Apple Pencil.
- **Start over:** tap **Clear** in the blue card.

<Tip title="Leave Tangent chain on when you can">Fillet then finds the whole run again each time the part changes. Round more corners earlier in the timeline, and the outline still gets rounded all the way round.</Tip>

## Choose the size

**Radius** and **Distance** take a number in millimetres, or an expression such as `wall / 2`. With a parameter in the field, the round follows when the parameter changes.

To change the size later, tap the step in the timeline, type the new size and tap **Done**.

<Tip title="Printing tip">Chamfer the bottom edges rather than round them. The lowest part of a fillet there faces almost straight down and prints as an overhang. A 45° chamfer prints without supports.</Tip>

## Hollow a part with Shell

Shell turns a solid into walls of one thickness. The faces you pick are taken away and leave the part open there.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap the face you want open, such as the top.
    2. Under **Next** in the inspector, tap **Shell, removing these faces**.
    3. Type the wall thickness in **Thickness**.
    4. Tap **Done**.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click the face you want open, such as the top.
    2. Under **Next** in the inspector, click **Shell, removing these faces**.
    3. Type the wall thickness in **Thickness**.
    4. Press <Keys combo="↩" />, or click **Done**.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

The walls grow inward, so the outside keeps its size.

- **Open another side:** tap the outside of that face. This works on a box with sharp corners. On a part with rounded corners, like this plate, an open side makes the step fail, so open only the top.
- **Close an opening:** an opening is a hole now, so there's nothing there to tap. Tap **Clear** in the blue card. That closes every opening, so tap the faces you still want open again.
- **No opening at all:** tap **Shell** in the tool strip with nothing selected, and pick no faces. You get a closed box with a hollow inside.

<Tip title="Printing tip">Make walls 2–3 nozzle widths thick. On a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.8–1.2 mm walls print cleanly.</Tip>

## When a step fails

A size can be too big for the part. A round can't be bigger than the faces beside its edge, and a wall can't be thicker than the part.

<Figure src="/screens/fillet-chamfer-shell/fail.png" width={1600} height={840} phoneSrc="/screens/fillet-chamfer-shell/fail-phone.png" phoneWidth={720} phoneHeight={1288} alt="Fillet with Radius 14 mm on the top outline of a plate 12 mm tall. A red card in the inspector reads Fillet radius too large for the selected edges, and the plate keeps its sharp top edges." caption="A 14 mm round can't fit on a plate 12 mm tall, so the edges stay sharp." />

When that happens, the part stays as it was and a red card at the top of the inspector says why. The step also turns red in the timeline, with a red dot on its corner.

<Figure src="/screens/fillet-chamfer-shell/fail-timeline.png" width={760} height={144} maxWidth={420} alt="The timeline: Outline, Plate, Corners, and Fillet 1 with a red label and a red dot." />

To fix it:

1. Make the size smaller until the red card goes away.
2. If one edge is the trouble, drop it and round it in a step of its own with a smaller size.
3. If Shell says the wall is too thick and a thinner wall doesn't help, an open side runs into a rounded corner. Tap **Clear** and pick only the top.

You can tap **Done** with the error still there. The step stays red in the timeline until you open it and fix it.

## Questions and answers

<FAQ>
  <QA q="Why did one tap pick so many edges?">**Tangent chain** is on. It picks every edge joined smoothly to the one you tapped. Turn it off to drop single edges.</QA>
  <QA q="Fillet or chamfer on the bottom of a part?">A chamfer. The lowest part of a fillet on a bottom edge faces almost straight down, so it prints as an overhang. A small 45° chamfer prints cleanly. Keep fillets for the top and the upright edges.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I round edges on two parts in one step?">No. A Fillet or Chamfer step works on one body. Make a second step for the other one.</QA>
  <QA q="How do I close a Shell opening again?">Open the Shell step from the timeline and tap **Clear** in the blue card. Every opening closes, and you can tap the faces you still want open again. The opening itself can't be tapped, because that face is gone.</QA>
  <QA q="How do I make a closed box with no opening?">Start Shell from the tool strip with nothing selected, and don't pick a face. You get walls on every side and an empty space inside.</QA>
</FAQ>
