## Start Measure

Measure reads sizes straight off the model. It works on any body you can see.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap <Ui icon="measure">Measure</Ui> on the tool strip, or press <Keys combo="I" /> on a keyboard.
    2. Tap a face, an edge or a corner of the model.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click <Ui icon="measure">Measure</Ui> on the tool strip, choose **Design › Measure**, or press <Keys combo="I" />.
    2. Click a face, an edge or a corner of the model.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

The inspector shows a card for what you picked. The hint over the model says what the next tap does.

<Note>Measure needs a body. If a step is open, tap **Done** or **Cancel** first. An open sketch is finished for you.</Note>

## Read a size

Each pick gets a card that names what it is, such as **Circular edge** or **Flat face**, and lists its sizes.

| You pick | You get |
| --- | --- |
| A straight edge | Length |
| A round edge, such as a hole's rim | Length, Radius and Diameter |
| A flat face | Area |
| A cylindrical or spherical face, such as a hole's wall | Area, Radius and Diameter |
| A corner (vertex) | Its X, Y and Z position |

Lengths are in mm and areas in mm², rounded to two decimals.

## Measure between two things

Pick a second thing to get the shortest distance between the two. Fillet draws it on the model as a dimension line.

1. Tap the first thing, for example the rim of a hole.
2. Tap the second, for example the end face.
3. Read **Distance** in the **Between** card. Scroll the inspector to see the rest of the card.

The **Between** card holds:

- **Distance**: the shortest gap between the two picks.
- **ΔX**, **ΔY** and **ΔZ**: how far apart the two closest points are along each axis.
- **Angle**: only when both picks are flat faces or straight edges. Two faces give the angle between the ways they face, so the top and bottom of a plate read 180°.

A third tap starts over with that pick. Tap a picked thing again to drop it. The two picks can be on different bodies.

<Note>When the two picks touch, Distance reads 0 mm and no line is drawn.</Note>

## Measure whole bodies

Switch to bodies to get a part's volume and outer size.

1. In the inspector, set **Pick** to **Bodies**.
2. Tap a body.

The card shows its **Volume** in cm³ and its **Size**: width × depth × height of the box around it, in mm. Tap a second body to get the gap between the two, such as between a box and its lid.

<Note>Switching between **Faces & edges** and **Bodies** clears your picks.</Note>

## Copy a value

Every value has a copy button on its right. It copies the value as shown, such as `53 mm` or `90°`.

Paste a length or an angle into any size field and Fillet reads the unit. On the Mac you can also select the text and copy it.

## Start over or finish

- **Clear** drops your picks and keeps Measure on.
- <Keys combo="Esc" /> clears your picks. Press it again to leave Measure.
- **Done** ends Measure. So do tapping <Ui icon="measure">Measure</Ui> again and starting another tool.

Measuring is never a step. It doesn't change your design and isn't part of undo.

If the model changes while you measure, because you undo or an agent adds a step, picks on that body are dropped. Pick them again.

<Tip title="Measure inside a part">A [section view](/guides/section-view/) stays cut while you measure. What the cut hides can't be picked, so you can reach faces inside the part, such as the two sides of a wall.</Tip>

## Check a distance without Measure

For a quick number you don't need the tool. Select two things and the inspector shows the distance between them.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap a face or an edge.
    2. Tap a second one. It's added to the selection.
    3. Read **Distance** in the inspector.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click a face or an edge.
    2. Shift-click a second one.
    3. Read **Distance** in the inspector.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

Need ΔX, ΔY, ΔZ or the angle? Press <Keys combo="I" />: your two picks carry over into Measure.

## Questions

<FAQ>
  <QA q="Why is there no Angle?">Angle shows only when both picks are flat faces or straight edges. A round edge or a curved face has no single direction.</QA>
  <QA q="Why did my picks disappear?">The body changed: you undid a step, or an agent added one. Measure drops picks rather than read them off a different shape.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I measure a sketch?">No. Measure picks the faces, edges and corners of bodies, not sketch lines.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I measure a hidden body?">No. Show it in the browser first. Measure picks only what you can see.</QA>
</FAQ>
