## Name a value

Parameters live at the bottom of the browser, the sidebar on the left. If it's hidden, tap <Ui>Browser</Ui> at the left end of the tool strip.

1. Under **Parameters**, tap <Ui>Add parameter</Ui>. A card appears, named `p1` and set to 10 mm.
2. Tap `p1`, type a name such as `wall`, and press Return.
3. Tap the field after **=** and type the value, such as `2`.

A name uses letters, digits and `_`, and can't be a unit or function such as `in` or `sin`. If Fillet can't use a name, it says why under the card and keeps the old one.

## Use it in any field

Type the name wherever a number goes, and mix in sums and units: `height - wall`, `width / 3`, `1 in + wall`.

1. Open a step: tap its station on the timeline, or start a new one.
2. Tap a field and type an expression with the name, such as `height - wall`.
3. Tap <Ui>Done</Ui>.

The field keeps what you typed and shows what it comes to underneath.

## Change it once

Change a parameter's value, and every step that uses it rebuilds. In the pen holder, set `hole_d` to 16 for fatter markers. The holes grow, and the block grows 2 mm around them to keep its walls. Nothing else needs touching.

<Tip title="Name what you'll tune">Make a parameter for each size you expect to change: a wall, a fit, a screw size. Screw holes made from a preset already share one, `clearance`, so you can [tune them for your printer](/guides/holes/#tune-the-fit-for-your-printer).</Tip>

### If a step turns red

A step remembers each profile by the spot you tapped. If a change moves a profile away from that spot, the step turns red on the timeline. Set `width` to 120, and the outer holes slide away: *Holes failed: A selected profile no longer exists — reselect it*.

## Rename or delete a parameter

To rename a parameter, tap its name and type a new one. Fillet rewrites every expression that uses it, so nothing breaks.

To delete one, tap the trash button on its card. Steps that used it turn red, with a message such as *Unknown parameter 'hole_d'*. Undo brings it back.
