## See them in the app

You don't need this page open to find a key. The app lists every shortcut next to its command.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Connect a keyboard to your iPad and open a design.
    2. Hold <Keys combo="⌘" />, or open the menu bar at the top of the screen.
    3. Look under **View**, **Design** and **Sketch**.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Open a design.
    2. Open **View**, **Design** or **Sketch** in the menu bar.
    3. Each command shows its keys on the right.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

While you type in a field, the single-letter keys stand aside, so a letter always lands in the field.

## Steps and undo

### What Esc does

Esc backs out one level at a time. Each press does the first of these that fits:

1. Stops choosing a plane for a sketch or a section.
2. Puts the section handles away. The cut stays.
3. In Measure, clears the picks. The next press ends Measure.
4. Stops typing a size on the canvas (Mac).
5. Drops the shape you're halfway through drawing.
6. Leaves the sketch, keeping every shape in it.
7. Turns Box select off.
8. Cancels the open step.
9. Clears the selection.

## Type sizes while you draw

This one is Mac only. While a shape is in progress, type its sizes right on the canvas instead of aiming the pointer.

1. Press <Keys combo="R" /> and click the rectangle's first corner.
2. Press <Keys combo="⇥" />, or just start typing a number.
3. Type the width, then press <Keys combo="⇥" /> and type the height.
4. Press <Keys combo="↩" /> to place the rectangle.

<Shortcuts>
  <Shortcut keys="⇥" note="Keeps what you typed and moves to the next size.">Next size</Shortcut>
  <Shortcut keys="⇧⇥">Previous size</Shortcut>
  <Shortcut keys="↩" note="Keeps the size and places the point.">Place</Shortcut>
  <Shortcut keys="⌫" note="Takes back a digit. In an empty size, frees it to follow the pointer again.">Edit or unlock</Shortcut>
  <Shortcut keys="Esc" note="The sizes you already locked stay.">Stop typing</Shortcut>
</Shortcuts>

A size can be a sum or a parameter, such as `wall * 2`. Line takes a length and an angle, and keeps going from the new point: type 40, press <Keys combo="⇥" />, type 90 and press <Keys combo="↩" />. That draws a 40 mm line at 90° and starts the next one.

<Note>Rectangle and Center Rectangle take a width and a height, Circle a diameter, Polygon a radius, Arc and Slot a length, and Offset a distance. On iPad, draw first, then type the sizes in the inspector.</Note>

## Questions

<FAQ>
  <QA q="Why does a letter key do nothing?">A text field has focus. While you type in a field, letters go into it and never start a tool. Leave the field first. If a step is open, a new tool waits until you tap **Done** or **Cancel**, and Fillet says so.</QA>
  <QA q="Does Esc throw my sketch away?">No. Esc leaves the sketch and keeps every shape. To take back a shape, press <Keys combo="⌘Z" /> while the sketch is open.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I change a shortcut?">On the Mac, yes. In System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › App Shortcuts, add one for Fillet with the menu command's exact name, such as `Revolve`.</QA>
</FAQ>
