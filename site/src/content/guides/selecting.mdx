## Pick edges and faces

A tap picks one thing. Picked edges and faces turn blue.

<Platforms>
  <Platform name="iPad">
    1. Tap a face or an edge to select it.
    2. Tap more to add them to the selection.
    3. Tap a picked one again to drop it.
    4. Tap empty space to clear the selection.
  </Platform>
  <Platform name="Mac">
    1. Click a face or an edge. A click replaces what was selected.
    2. Shift-click or ⌘-click to add more, or to drop one.
    3. Click empty space to clear the selection.
  </Platform>
</Platforms>

A tap close to an edge picks the edge. To pick the face, tap further inside it.

<Note>Selecting is never an undo step. Undo takes back your last change to the design, not your last pick.</Note>

## See what you picked

The inspector on the right describes each picked item:

- **A face:** its shape and area, such as "Flat face, Area 3908.75 mm²".
- **An edge:** its shape and length. A circular edge also shows its diameter.
- **A body:** its name and size, such as "80 × 50 × 8 mm".

Pick exactly two things and the inspector shows the shortest **Distance** between them. With more than four picked, it lists four and counts the rest.

## Go on to the next step

Under **Next**, the inspector lists the steps that fit your selection. Tap one to start it on what you picked.

| You picked | Next offers |
| --- | --- |
| Edges | **Fillet edges**, **Chamfer edges** |
| A flat face | **Sketch on this face**, **Drill holes in this face**, **Lay flat on the bed** |
| Any faces | **Press Pull these faces**, **Shell, removing these faces** |

To start over, tap **Clear selection** at the bottom of the list.

## Pick a whole loop or chain

You can pick a row of edges without tapping each one.

- **Double-tap an edge** to select the loop of the face beside it: the face's whole outline. Tap on the side of the face you mean. On a Mac, double-click.
- **Select more › Tangent chain** adds every edge joined smoothly to the last edge you picked. On a plate with rounded corners, one top edge grows into the whole rounded outline.
- **Select more › Loop** adds the loop around the face of the last edge you picked, on the face turned most towards you.

**Select more** shows in the inspector whenever an edge is picked. On a Mac, the same commands are in the **Design** menu.

## Select many at once with a box

A box picks everything in an area in one drag.

1. Tap <Ui>Box select</Ui> in the tool strip, or press <Keys combo="W" />. A bar appears over the model.
2. In the bar, choose **Edges**, **Faces** or **Bodies**.
3. Drag with one finger. Drag **right** to take only what is wholly inside the box. Drag **left** to take anything the box touches.
4. Check what lights up and the count on the box's tag, then lift your finger.

<Figure src="/screens/selecting/box-select-crossing.png" width={1584} height={1286} alt="A dashed selection box dragged leftwards over one end of the plate. A tag on the box reads Touching 7, and the edges it touches are lit blue, including the long edges that run out of the box." caption="Dragged left, the box takes every edge it touches, even edges that run out of it." />

The box shows which kind it is. A box dragged right has a solid rim and its tag says **Inside**. A box dragged left has a dashed rim and says **Touching**.

On iPad, what a box takes is added to the selection. When you lift your finger, Box select turns off, so one finger orbits again. To turn it off without boxing, tap **Done** or press <Keys combo="W" /> or <Keys combo="Esc" />.

<Tip title="Apple Pencil">Apple Pencil draws a box any time, without turning on Box select. Your fingers keep turning the view.</Tip>

A box takes only what you can see. It skips hidden bodies and anything behind a face. To get the back of a part, turn the view first.

Box select isn't available in a sketch, while choosing a plane, while placing holes or while measuring.

**On a Mac,** Shift-drag or ⌘-drag draws a box any time. A plain box replaces the selection, a Shift box adds to it, and a ⌘ box adds or drops. With Box select on, a left-drag draws a box and Box select stays on. Right-drag to orbit.

## Pick bodies

A tap in the view picks faces and edges, never a whole body. To pick a body:

- Tap its row under **Bodies** in the browser. This picks that body alone. Tap the row again to drop it.
- Or draw a box with **Bodies** chosen in the box bar.

The inspector shows the body's name and its size.

## Pick for a step

Steps such as Fillet, Chamfer, Shell and Press Pull ask you to pick edges or faces. While a step picks, the rules change a little:

- Every tap adds or drops, on iPad and on Mac.
- A box takes the kind of thing the step needs. The bar names it, such as "Drag across edges", and has no Edges, Faces or Bodies choice.
- A box adds to the step's picks. Hold <Keys combo="⌘" /> while you drag to box picks out again.
- A double tap on an edge picks its loop for the step.

Fillet and Chamfer start with **Tangent chain** on, so one tap picks every edge joined smoothly to the one you tapped.

What you pick in a step belongs to that step. **Cancel** throws it away with the step.

## Keys

<Shortcuts>
  <Shortcut keys="W" note="Off again with W, Esc or Done.">Box select</Shortcut>
  <Shortcut keys="Esc" note="Turns Box select off. With nothing else open, clears the selection.">Stop or clear</Shortcut>
  <Shortcut keys="Shift / ⌘" note="Held while you tap or click: adds or drops. A tap on empty space then keeps the selection.">Add or drop</Shortcut>
</Shortcuts>

On iPad with a trackpad or mouse, a click replaces the selection, as on a Mac. Finger and Pencil taps add.

## Questions

<FAQ>
  <QA q="Why does a tap add instead of starting over?">On iPad, taps add, because touch has no Shift key. Tap empty space or **Clear selection** first.</QA>
  <QA q="Why didn't my box take the edges at the back?">A box only takes what you can see. Turn the view so those edges face you, then box again.</QA>
  <QA q="Why does one finger turn the view again after my box?">A finger box turns Box select off when you lift your finger. Press <Keys combo="W" /> or tap <Ui>Box select</Ui> to draw another.</QA>
  <QA q="Can I undo a selection?">No. Selecting isn't an undo step, so Undo skips it and takes back your last change to the design.</QA>
</FAQ>
