## Sections and steps

Each section opens with one short paragraph that says what the reader will get. Then come numbered steps, no more than five, each one imperative line. Name app controls the way the app shows them: <Ui icon="holes">Hole</Ui>, <Ui icon="extrude-revolve-press-pull">Extrude</Ui>, or plain **Done**.

1. Select the top face of the plate.
2. Tap <Ui icon="holes">Hole</Ui> in the tool strip, or press <Keys combo="H" />.
3. Choose **Socket head counterbore** › **M3** from the preset menu.
4. Tap the face where each screw goes.
5. Tap **Done**.

<Figure src="/screens/sample-viewport.png" alt="Four parts on the print bed" caption="A filleted block, a T-section, a cylinder and a plate with a hole, on the A1 mini bed." width={1600} height={1120} />

<Note>One Hole step drills one face. To drill another face, start a new Hole step.</Note>

### Key caps

Undo with <Keys combo="⌘Z" /> and redo with <Keys combo="⇧⌘Z" />. Finish a sketch with <Keys combo="⌘↩" />.
