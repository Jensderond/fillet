## Scores

### The rubric's five dimensions

| Dimension | Mac | iPad | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Philosophy alignment** | 7 | 7 | A coherent "technical drawing" direction: navy ink, orange only for "active", blue only for "picked", condensed drafting labels, a ruler timeline with a caliper and section hatching. It loses a point because the icon and splash's cream graph paper disappears in the editor: the chrome is cool slate, and the viewport and view cube are generic grey. |
| **Visual hierarchy** | 7 | 6 | Each panel has a clear title, one orange primary action and an ink hint pill. Several things compete or go missing: two orange "Finish sketch" buttons, rolled-back steps that look almost like active ones, a grey model on a grey bed, and selected edges you can barely see. | **Craft quality** | 7 | 6 | Tokens are disciplined: 4 pt grid, 3 radii, measured contrast, Increase Contrast variants, press scale on every pressable. | **Functionality** | 7 | 6 | The editing model is strong: live preview, expression fields, a print check, Cancel and Done, and a Pencil-draws / finger-navigates split. Several things hurt it. On iPad, half of the sketch tools sit behind a horizontal scroll in every orientation. |
| **Originality** | 7 | 7 | The ruler and caliper timeline, the hatched rolled-back steps, the plotter-drawn splash and the printer-aware print check would not be mistaken for a template. Outside those moments the chrome is well-made but conventional SwiftUI. There are no slop patterns. |
| **Average** | **7.0** | **6.4** | **Combined: 6.7** |

## What it would take to reach 8+ on every dimension

This list is ordered and kept minimal. Each step names the dimension it lifts. Steps 1–4 bring the iPad to the 7 bar. Steps 5–8 are what makes it an 8.

1. **Give the sketch tools their room** (R01, R09). One primary Finish, a lead-aligned bar and grouped tools, so all 10 tools are visible on a 13" iPad in both orientations. *iPad Functionality 6→7, Hierarchy 6→7.*
2. **Make the camera content-aware** (R02). Frame the sketch on entry, fit the first body, and keep the model centred when the inspector column comes and goes. *iPad Functionality and Hierarchy +1, Mac Hierarchy +½.*
3. **Tell the truth about state** (R03, R04, R05). Live Undo/Redo, tools that show availability and the reason, and failed steps announced with a "Show" jump. *Functionality +1 on both platforms (Mac 7→8).*
4. **Make rollback and picks unmistakable** (R07, R10, R21). A dashed rolled-back segment plus a "Showing step 2 of 5" pill, and thick haloed picked edges. *Hierarchy 7→8 on the Mac, and the second step for the iPad.*
5. **Close the craft gaps** (R06, R08, R17, R18, R20, R25). Mac hover parity, platform-correct verbs, one themed segmented control, and no UUIDs or orphaned wraps. *Craft 7→8 (Mac), 6→7→8 (iPad, with R19).*
6. **Bring the paper into the workspace** (R11, R12). A drafting-paper bed, a brand-toned model with a contact shadow, and a view cube in tokens, so splash, icon and editor read as one object. *Philosophy 7→8, Originality 7→8.*
7. **Make touch fluid** (R13, R14). Orbit and pan momentum with velocity hand-off, and a draggable drawer with velocity-projected detents. *iPad motion 6→8; supports iPad Functionality 8.*
8. **Finish accessibility** (R15, R16, R19, R22, R24). Two contrast fixes, an accessibility-size inspector layout, and a described 3D view. *Accessibility 7→8.*

## Keep as is

- **The colour semantics.** Orange means "active / you are here" (current tool, step being edited, caliper, primary button). Blue means "picked in the model", consistent from viewport to inspector card to "Clear". Ink is for everything else, and toggles stay ink rather than orange. It's rare to see this held so strictly.
- **The token file** (`Theme.swift`). Contrast ratios are stated next to each colour and they hold up: `inkSecondary` 5.37:1, white on `filletFill` 4.56:1, `selectInk` on `selectSoft` 4.75:1. Increase Contrast variants, a 4 pt spacing scale, three radii and hit-target metrics by input type complete it.
- **The ruler timeline.** Stations on a graduated rule, a caliper you drag 1:1 with selection ticks, hatching for rolled-back steps, drag-to-reorder that refuses illegal moves with a reason, and VoiceOver adjustable and custom actions. It is the app's identity.

- **`ExprField`.** Expressions with live validation, an inline error, an "= 12 mm" result line, the unit inside the field, a field-coloured focus ring, and bare-key shortcuts withdrawn while typing.
- **`SelectionCount`.** A dashed "Pick edges" well that becomes a blue "4 edges picked · Clear" card. It is an elegant empty-to-filled state.
- **The responsive layout.** Regular, medium and compact breakpoints. A floating browser at medium width, a docked inspector at compact width instead of a form sheet that would cover the model, and the inspector always visible at regular width so the viewport doesn't jump.
